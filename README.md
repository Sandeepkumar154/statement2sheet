# 📊 Statement2Sheet — Every Financial & PDF Tool in One Place

> **The 100% in-browser, privacy-first PDF and Bank Statement suite. Zero server uploads, zero data exfiltration, $0 hosting cost forever.**

---

## 🌟 What is Statement2Sheet?
Statement2Sheet combines all standard PDF tools (Merge, Split, Organize, Rotate, Unlock, Watermark, Page Numbers, Compress, PDF to JPG) with an **enterprise-grade bank statement extraction and balance reconciliation engine** that converts bank statements from any country, currency, or layout into audited 3-sheet Excel and CSV files.

---

## 🛠️ Complete 24-Tool Financial & PDF Suite
### 💼 Financial & Accounting Core
1. 📊 **Bank Statement to Excel (.xlsx) & CSV**: Universal semantic parser handling Indian Lakhs, European dates, century ambiguity, and multi-check grids with mathematical balance reconciliation.
2. ⚡ **QuickBooks (.QBO / OFX) Export**: Direct Intuit WebConnect format output for direct QuickBooks and accounting software imports.
3. 📝 **Word (.doc) Ledger & Markdown**: Clean formatted accounting summary exports with transaction badges and category totals.
4. 🛡️ **Audit Inspector & Balance Reconciler**: In-depth balance verification, mathematical check calculations, and duplicate detection.

### 📑 PDF Document Organization & Editing
5. 📑 **Merge PDF**: Combine multiple monthly bank statements or documents with visual cover previews and expandable page galleries.
6. ✂️ **Split PDF**: Extract specific pages with live high-resolution thumbnails, 3-way view size toggles, and 🔍 full-screen zoom inspection.
7. 🔄 **Organize PDF**: Rearrange, delete, and re-order pages dynamically.
8. 📐 **Crop PDF**: Precision interactive margin trimming and white space removal.
9. 🔁 **Batch Rotate PDF**: Rotate individual pages or entire documents with live angle preview.
10. 🏷️ **Watermark PDF**: Stamp custom text watermarks with custom angle, opacity, and typography.
11. 🔢 **Page Numbers**: Add customizable, formatted page numbers across documents.

### 🔄 Multi-Format Conversions
12. 🖼️ **PDF to Image**: Render high-resolution PNG/JPG images from PDF pages.
13. 📸 **Image to PDF**: Combine smartphone receipts and document photos into a unified PDF.
14. 📄 **PDF to Word (.docx)**: Layout-preserving OpenXML (.docx) generation with structured paragraphs and headings.
15. 📊 **Office & HTML to PDF**: Convert Excel sheets (.xlsx, .csv), HTML, and TXT files directly to paginated PDFs in browser.
16. 🔍 **Lossless Image Extractor**: Scan and extract embedded raster photos and vector graphics from PDF pages.
17. 📜 **PDF to Markdown**: Fast text extraction to clean markdown with table formatting.

### 🛡️ Security, Privacy & Compliance
18. 🗜️ **Compress PDF**: Optimize file size with configurable quality levels while retaining clarity.
19. 🔒 **Protect PDF**: Client-side AES/Standard encryption with password protection.
20. 🔓 **Unlock PDF**: Decrypt password-protected PDFs locally in browser RAM.
21. ✍️ **Sign PDF (Visual)**: Canvas pen drawing, digital signature placement, and multi-color stamps.
22. 🔏 **Digital PKI Sign (WebCrypto)**: High-security SHA-256 / RSA-PSS cryptographic signatures with verifiable audit receipts.
23. 🏛️ **PDF/A ISO 19005-1 Archival**: Inject standardized XMP metadata and sRGB OutputIntents for long-term document preservation.
24. 🖤 **Permanent PDF Redaction**: Deep raster flattening that permanently obliterates underlying sensitive text, account numbers, and metadata.

### 🤖 Local Intelligent Analysis
* 🧠 **Document Summarizer**: In-browser extractive TF-IDF summarizer with currency and financial entity extraction.

---

## 🔒 100% Client-Side Privacy
* Zero document bytes, account numbers, or balances leave your device.
* All parsing, OCR, and PDF assembly execute in browser memory via WebAssembly and HTML5 TypedArrays.
* Immune to server-side data breaches.
